Milestone 1 — Redesign the Foundation ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Before adding twenty more project pages, I’d make sure the overall experience is solid.

Things I’d improve:

* Cleaner color palette
* Better typography
* Better spacing
* Consistent buttons
* Consistent cards
* Better navigation
* Better project page template

Regarding the gradient…

“…I use a gradient background that is green, blue, and purple.”

Gradients aren’t inherently unprofessional. The question is how they’re used.

For example:

❌ Entire page is a bright rainbow gradient

vs.

✅ White/light gray page with a subtle blue gradient hero section

Many professional companies use gradients:

* Stripe
* Linear
* Vercel
* Discord
* Microsoft

The trick is subtlety.

For a portfolio, I’d lean toward something like:

* White or light gray page background
* Navy or dark charcoal text
* One accent color (blue or teal)
* Use your gradient only in the hero/banner

That immediately feels much more polished.

Milestone 2 — Responsive Design ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I’d honestly move this ahead of adding lots of projects.

Imagine a recruiter opens your portfolio on their phone.

If everything breaks…

…they may never even look at your projects.

Responsive design isn’t glamorous, but it has a huge impact.

Things to check:

* Navigation collapses nicely
* Cards stack vertically
* Images resize correctly
* Text remains readable
* Buttons are easy to tap

⸻
